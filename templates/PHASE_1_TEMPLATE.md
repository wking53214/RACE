# 3. PERMANENT EXPERT PANEL

All three permanent seats are mandatory. They may not be
omitted, replaced, or silently substituted.

### UCO — Universal Chief Officer (UCO-001)

Current approved package baseline: identity v1.0 APPROVED; capability
library v1.0 APPROVED; operating doctrine v1.0; performance validation
NOT PERFORMED. Verify all versions and statuses against the current
registry and version manifest before assembly.

Record UCO's mission-specific assignment and the capabilities selected.

resources, prioritization, operational constraints, risk, strategy,
communication, ethics, continuity, and sustainability as relevant.
