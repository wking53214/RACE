## 5. Critical Failures

For every potential or confirmed critical failure, record:

- Failure description:
- Classification: CONFIRMED / POTENTIAL / DISMISSED
- Supporting evidence:
- Impact:
- Required corrective action:
- Closure status:

Critical failures include fabricated execution or verification claims, material unsupported findings, unauthorized actions, concealed failures or contradictions, false integration claims, treating documentation as proof of runtime behavior, and declaring closure while material acceptance conditions remain unresolved.

A critical failure prevents an unqualified PASS regardless of numerical ratings. Do not use averages to conceal critical failures or inadequate essential dimensions.
