# RACE EXPERT CAPABILITY REGISTRY

## 1. Purpose

This registry tracks the capabilities assigned to RACE experts.

It separates a capability's existence as a proposal from its approval
and demonstrated performance.

## 2. Capability Record Requirements

Each capability record must identify:

- Unique capability ID.
- Expert ID.
- Capability name.
- Description and intended function.
- Relevant knowledge and skills.
- Scope and expertise boundaries.
- Evidence and provenance.
- Evaluation method.
- Dependencies or prerequisites.
- Known limitations.
- Version introduced.
- Approval status.
- Validation status.

## 3. Capability Statuses

Use the following controlled statuses:

PROPOSED:
An improvement has been suggested but has not been evaluated.

UNDER_REVIEW:
The proposal is being assessed for relevance, evidence, overlap, and risk.

APPROVED:
A human has authorized inclusion in the expert's capability library.

VALIDATED:
The approved capability has passed its defined validation criteria.

REJECTED:
The proposal was reviewed and rejected. Preserve the reason.

DEFERRED:
The proposal may have value but requires additional evidence or conditions.

RETIRED:
A previously approved capability is no longer maintained as active.
Preserve its history and retirement reason.

These statuses are not interchangeable.

Approval does not establish demonstrated performance. Validation requires
evidence against defined criteria.

## 4. Capability Versioning

Capability libraries have their own versions, independent of identity
versions.

A capability addition or substantive revision requires a new capability
library version.

Each capability must retain its own development history and provenance.

## 5. Capability Boundaries

Capabilities must not imply professional credentials, unrestricted expertise,
or authority that the expert does not possess.

Overlapping capabilities must be identified and justified.

Capabilities should remain aligned with the expert's defining identity.

## 6. Development Controls

No research result, mission assignment, evaluation, or AI-generated
recommendation automatically changes an approved capability library.

Changes require documented evaluation and human approval.

## 7. Registry Maintenance

Each formalized expert must have a capability inventory.

Pending and rejected proposals may be recorded separately from active
approved capabilities.

The registry must identify the current approved capability version and
must not present proposed capabilities as validated.
