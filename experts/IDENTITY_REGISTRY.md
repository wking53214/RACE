# RACE EXPERT IDENTITY REGISTRY

## 1. Purpose

This registry identifies the canonical permanent and specialist expert
identities maintained by RACE.

It is the authoritative index of expert identity versions.

## 3. Identity Record Requirements

Every formalized expert identity must have:

- Unique expert ID.
- Canonical name.
- Identity version.
- Classification: permanent or specialist.
- Purpose and defining perspective.
- Professional and intellectual orientation.
- Distinctive thinking and investigative methods.
- Strengths and limitations.
- Expertise boundaries.
- Relationship to other panel members.
- Behavioral expectations.
- Approval and publication history.

## 4. Identity Versioning

Identity versions use MAJOR.MINOR notation.

MAJOR changes alter fundamental identity, defining perspective, or role.

MINOR changes clarify or extend identity without changing its fundamental
character.

Every substantive identity change requires explicit human approval.

Prior versions must remain reconstructable.

## 5. Identity Stability

An expert's identity must not change simply because:
- A mission has different subject matter.
- A temporary role requires different emphasis.
- A capability is added.
- A new research finding becomes available.
- Another panel member disagrees with it.

Identity changes require a separate documented decision.

## 6. Registration Rules

New experts must receive unique IDs.

Existing IDs must never be reassigned to different identities.

A specialist does not become a permanent panel member merely because it
participates in one or more missions.
