# RACE EXPERT LIBRARY

## 1. Purpose

The RACE Expert Library maintains persistent, versioned expert identities and
their approved capabilities.

It exists to ensure that permanent RACE panel members remain recognizable,
functionally distinct, and consistently represented across missions while
allowing their capabilities to improve through controlled development.

An expert is more than a job title or a temporary mission role.

Each permanent expert has:
- A stable identity.
- A defined professional and intellectual orientation.
- A distinctive analytical perspective.
- Explicit capabilities and expertise boundaries.
- An established relationship with other panel members.
- A governed development history.
- Versioned identity and capability records.

## 2. Core Architecture

The library separates three things:

1. IDENTITY: Who the expert is and what makes its perspective distinct.
2. CAPABILITIES: What the expert is equipped to analyze or contribute.
3. OPERATING DOCTRINE: How the expert applies its identity and capabilities.

These components are related but must not be treated as interchangeable.

Identity should remain stable. Capabilities may develop through evidence,
evaluation, and explicit human approval. Operating doctrine governs how
capabilities are applied.

## 3. Permanent Panel

Every RACE mission must retain these three permanent expert seats:

- UCO — Universal Chief Officer.
- The Hacker.
- MacG.

These seats cannot be removed, replaced, or silently redefined.

Mission-specific specialists may supplement the permanent panel when
their contributions are justified by the mission.

## 4. Versioning

Identity and capabilities have independent version numbers.

Example:

- Expert identity: UCO Identity v1.0
- Capability library: UCO Capabilities v1.3
- Operating doctrine: UCO Operating Doctrine v1.0

A capability addition does not automatically change identity.

An identity revision requires separate justification, review, and explicit
human approval.

Substantive revisions create new versions. Historical versions must remain
available for reconstruction of prior RACE prompts.

## 5. Development Governance

No expert may autonomously promote a proposed capability into its approved
capability library.

Development must distinguish:

- Observations.
- Research findings.
- Proposed improvements.
- Evaluated capabilities.
- Approved capabilities.
- Validated capabilities.

A mission result or isolated observation does not automatically establish
a permanent expert capability.

Changes require:
1. A documented proposal.
2. Supporting evidence and provenance.
3. Relevance to the expert's identity.
4. Assessment of overlap and unintended consequences.
5. Appropriate evaluation.
6. Explicit human approval.
7. Versioned publication.
8. Validation of the resulting change.

## 6. Mission-Specific Context

Mission assignments may determine which capabilities receive emphasis.

They do not automatically change an expert's permanent identity or
capability library.

Temporary mission instructions must remain distinguishable from permanent
expert definitions.

## 7. Evidence and Honesty

The library must distinguish demonstrated capabilities from claims,
intentions, assumptions, and unverified proposals.

A written capability description does not prove that an AI system can
reliably perform that capability.

A simulated expert perspective must not be represented as an independently
executed AI agent or independent review.

## 8. Human Authority

The Expert Library does not grant experts real-world authority.

It does not authorize repository modifications, external actions, financial
commitments, deployments, or other actions.

Human authorization remains controlling.

## 9. Governing Documents

- IDENTITY_REGISTRY.md — permanent expert identity inventory.
- CAPABILITY_REGISTRY.md — capability inventory and status.
- DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md — controlled expert development history.
- ASSEMBLY_PROTOCOL.md — rules for assembling experts into RACE prompts.

Individual expert definitions reside under permanent/ or specialists/.

Mission snapshots preserve the exact expert definitions used in a campaign.
