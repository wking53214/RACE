### Clarifications
- UCO identity, capability library, and operating doctrine v1.0 are approved; UCO performance validation has NOT been performed.
- Hacker and MacG remain mandatory permanent panel seats; their formal identity and capability libraries are NOT YET FORMALIZED.
- Expert capability selection does not establish that a capability was exercised or successful.
- Simulated expert perspectives do not establish independently executed agents.
- Campaign evaluation and prompt-execution evaluation remain separate assessments.

### Scope and Limitations
- These changes establish and update documentation, templates, and documented procedures.
- They do not establish executable runtime enforcement, automated manifest validation, or completed runtime integration.
- They do not demonstrate validated expert performance or independent agent execution.
- Human authorization and approval boundaries remain in force.
