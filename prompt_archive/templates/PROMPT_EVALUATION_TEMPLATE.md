## 4. Prompt Execution Evaluation Dimensions

Rate each dimension from 0 to 5, or N/A when genuinely not applicable.

- 0 — NOT ADDRESSED
- 1 — INADEQUATE
- 2 — PARTIALLY ADEQUATE
- 3 — ADEQUATE
- 4 — STRONG
- 5 — EXCEPTIONAL
- N/A — NOT APPLICABLE

Every rating requires rationale and evidence references. Identify evidence
precisely. If evidence is unavailable, say so.

### 4.1 Mission Alignment

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did the prompt accurately translate the human objective into mission,
scope, constraints, and deliverables?

### 4.2 Instruction Clarity

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Were instructions understandable, consistent, sufficiently specific,
and free of material ambiguity?

### 4.3 Technical Completeness

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did the prompt include necessary technical context, requirements,
interfaces, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and output expectations?

### 4.4 Evidence Discipline

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did the prompt require accurate distinction among EXECUTED, INSPECTED,
INFERRED, and UNKNOWN, and prevent unsupported claims?

### 4.5 Adversarial Effectiveness

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did the prompt challenge assumptions, expose failure modes, test
alternatives, and address likely attacks or unintended consequences?

### 4.6 Expert Panel Effectiveness

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Were all permanent seats represented, assignments distinct, and
contributions and limitations accurately reported? Were additional
specialists justified where needed?

### 4.7 Authorization Compliance

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Were permitted actions, prohibited actions, approval requirements, and
human authority boundaries explicit and respected?

### 4.8 Deliverable Quality

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did execution produce required deliverables with expected completeness,
accuracy, usability, and traceability?

### 4.9 Handoff Quality

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Could another person or system understand results, evidence, open issues,
limitations, and next steps without unstated context?

### 4.10 Efficiency

- Rating:
- Rationale:
- Evidence references:

Did the prompt achieve its purpose without unnecessary complexity,
duplicated work, avoidable tool use, or disproportionate effort?
